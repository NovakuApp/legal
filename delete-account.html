Delete Your Account

Effective date: 06 June 2026 · Version 2.1.0

Follow the instructions below for your NOVAKU app to delete your account and associated data.

NOVAKU: History

NOVAKU: History

Step-by-step: Delete all local progress

  1. Open NOVAKU: History on your device.
  2. Tap Profile (bottom navigation, rightmost icon).
  3. Tap Reset all progress.
  4. Confirm the action. All local quiz scores, XP, streaks, badges, and achievements are immediately deleted from your device.

Step-by-step: Sign out and remove account from device

  1. Open NOVAKU: History on your device.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. Tap Sign Out. Your email address, profile data, and session are removed from the device.

Request permanent cloud data deletion

Signing out removes your data from your device, but your data may still be stored in our cloud database (Supabase). To permanently delete your cloud data:

  1. Send an email to novakuapp@gmail.com with the subject line: "Data Deletion Request — NOVAKU: History".
  2. Include the email address associated with your account.
  3. We will process your request and permanently delete your data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • All quiz scores, lesson progress, XP, and streaks
  • All unlocked badges and achievements
  • Your account credentials and profile data stored on our servers
Fan Mosaic

Fan Mosaic

Step-by-step: Delete your account and photo

  1. Open Fan Mosaic on your device.
  2. Tap Profile (bottom navigation, rightmost icon).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the action in the dialog.

What happens immediately

  • Your account credentials and profile data are removed from the device.
  • Your photograph is permanently deleted from cloud storage (Supabase, Frankfurt).
  • Your fan profile is removed from the leaderboard.
  • Your wc_fans row is deleted from our database; the proof-of-consent record (privacy_* columns) is retained as required by §6 of the Privacy Policy (6 years post-deletion, for GDPR Art. 7.1 evidence).

Important: Mosaic regeneration

Your photo may continue to appear in previously generated mosaic images (composite JPEGs) until the next scheduled regeneration cycle. Mosaics are regenerated daily at approximately 02:00 UTC. After that cycle completes, your photo will no longer appear in any mosaic.

This is a technical limitation of composite image generation: previously rendered mosaics cannot be retroactively modified. However, your photo is fully removed from storage and will not appear in any future regeneration.

Request permanent cloud data deletion

If you want to confirm permanent deletion of your cloud data, or if you are unable to use the in-app deletion flow:

  1. Send an email to novakuapp@gmail.com with subject: "Data Deletion Request — Fan Mosaic".
  2. Include the email address or display name associated with your account.
  3. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your photograph from cloud storage (immediately on app deletion, or within 30 days of email request)
  • Your display name and fan profile data
  • Your approval status and submission history
  • Your account credentials
Novaku Flag Quiz

Novaku Flag Quiz

Step-by-step: Delete your account and all data

  1. Open Novaku Flag Quiz on your device.
  2. Tap Profile (bottom navigation, rightmost icon).
  3. Tap Settings, then Account.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm the action in the two-step confirmation dialog.

What happens immediately

  • Your anonymous Supabase account (or Google-linked account, if you upgraded) is permanently deleted from the server. Our fq-account-delete Edge Function runs synchronously and is the authoritative source of truth.
  • All your Flag Quiz data is purged: streaks, XP, levels, daily attempts, sprint and survival session histories, achievement unlocks, region mastery, Confusables training state, push notification tokens, and ad reward audit log.
  • All local data on your device is wiped: 5 local databases (app state, daily cache, country cache, session state, settings preferences) are cleared.
  • You are returned to the onboarding screen. Re-opening the app creates a brand-new anonymous account with no connection to the deleted one.

What is retained, and why

  • Proof-of-consent record: the timestamp, version, and SHA-256 hash of the Privacy Policy you accepted are retained for 6 years post-deletion, as required by §6 of the Privacy Policy (GDPR Art. 7.1 evidence).
  • Anonymised aggregate counters: total play counts, completion rates, and similar metrics that contain no personal data may continue to exist in aggregated form.
  • Crash reports: any crash logs captured by Sentry are automatically deleted after 90 days; we cannot retrieve or attribute them to your account once your user ID is deleted.

Pro subscription holders

Deleting your in-app account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. If you have an active Novaku Pro subscription (novaku_pro_annual or flagquiz_pro_lifetime), you must cancel it separately through Google Play:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Find Novaku Flag Quiz or Novaku Pro and tap Cancel subscription.

A one-time lifetime purchase cannot be refunded automatically; contact novakuapp@gmail.com within the Play Store's refund window if you need a refund.

Request permanent cloud data deletion by email

If you cannot use the in-app deletion flow (uninstalled the app, lost device access, etc.):

  1. Send an email to novakuapp@gmail.com with subject: "Data Deletion Request — Novaku Flag Quiz".
  2. Include any identifier you remember: the email address you used (if you upgraded with Google Sign-In), the approximate date you started playing, or the device model. Anonymous-only accounts are identified by the device's anonymous Supabase user ID, which we may not be able to recover without your help.
  3. We will process your request and permanently delete your data within 30 days, as required by GDPR Art. 17.

What gets deleted

  • All Daily Challenge attempts, scores, and streak history
  • All Classic, Sprint, and Survival session records
  • All Confusables training progress
  • All achievements, region mastery, and level/XP state
  • All push notification tokens registered to your account
  • All rewarded-ad claim audit entries
  • Your anonymous Supabase user ID (or Google-linked account, if upgraded)
  • All local preferences and caches on your device
Need help? Contact us at novakuapp@gmail.com — we aim to respond within 30 days.
